LANDING PAGE EXPERIENCE
RECOMMENDATIONS

The content on your residential LP is strong — great messaging, real testimonials, and a clear value prop. With 90%+ of ad spend driving mobile traffic, there are a few ways to improve the overall experience and tighten the conversion path.

Recommendation 1
CONDENSE TESTIMONIALS INTO A CAROUSEL
The testimonials are great — real reviews from real people. On mobile, all six are stacked vertically which adds a lot of scroll. A carousel would keep all the social proof visible without the extra height.
1
6 Stacked Cards → Swipeable Carousel
On mobile, six stacked review cards take up over 3 full screens of scrolling. On desktop, the 3+3 grid works better but still takes two viewport heights. A carousel with dots and arrows would keep every review accessible in a single screen height on both devices.

Recommendation 3
CONSOLIDATE OVERLAPPING SECTIONS

3

Recommendation 4
REDUCE REDUNDANT CTA BREAKS
The call-to-action blocks are solid — just appearing more often than needed. Trimming from 6 to 3 strategic placements would reduce scroll without losing conversion opportunities.
4

Recommendation 5
ADD INSURANCE VERIFICATION FORM TO DESKTOP HERO
On desktop, the hero section has a strong headline and CTA buttons — but there's an opportunity to capture leads right above the fold with an inline insurance verification form.
5
Desktop Hero → Add Inline Form
The current desktop hero has the headline on the left with empty space on the right. Adding an insurance verification form directly in the hero gives visitors an immediate action to take — no scrolling needed. We mocked this up previously and it fits naturally into the existing layout.
